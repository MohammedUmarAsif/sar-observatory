Mathematics to code

Coordinate system and dimensions

Cartesian coordinates in metres: platform p(η)=(vη,0,h); scatterer q=(x,y,0). x is along track, y is ground range and z is up. η is slow time in seconds; t is fast time in seconds. The ground is flat, the platform is straight and velocity constant. This is a stop-and-go model: motion during pulse propagation is neglected. Image arrays are [y,x], raw arrays [pulse,fast-time sample]. Sampling a complex baseband signal does not mean sampling a real carrier at its microwave frequency.

The resolution factors assume rectangular weighting and approximately linear spatial phase near broadside. They are half-power full widths; c/(2B) and λR/(2L) without 0.886 are characteristic first-null spacings, not identical width conventions. Finite chirp duration, interpolation, discrete aperture, finite measurement windows and ground projection explain numerical differences.

FFT and conjugation

The matched filter reverses time and conjugates complex phase. fftconvolve implements linear convolution, including sufficient padding. It is not a bare circular FFT product. An odd-length reference chirp preserves centre alignment in same mode. NumPy's default FFT is unnormalized forward, inverse normalized by transform length; changing normalization requires adjusting gain. np.vdot(template, raw) conjugates its first argument and serves as the separate delay-specific reference calculation. Complex interpolation applies to real and imaginary components, never to phase angles wrapped at ±π.

Impulse measurement conventions

P=|I|². Width crosses P/Ppeak=0.5 with linear interpolation on each side. No interpolation-based increase in physical resolving power is claimed. Cuts pass through the 2D peak; localization uses the nearest image-grid sample relative to known isolated-target coordinates. The discretization allowance is 2dx for widths; the location is quantized to dx/2 and dy/2 per coordinate. First neighboring local minima define the mainlobe; outside it, all samples in the supplied window contribute to ISLR. Windows are x ±8 m and y ±30 m. These are 1D cut metrics, not 2D sidelobe specifications. Discrete minima may not coincide with exact analytic nulls, especially with noise or severe defocus.

Noise, speckle and uncertainty

Injected circular complex Gaussian noise has E|n|²=σ²: each quadrature variance is σ²/2. Record SNR is mean clean echo power over all recorded samples divided by injected noise power. It is not focused target SNR or noise-equivalent sigma zero. Independent seeded repetitions assess variability, not scene-wide speckle statistics. The extended patch has random complex scatterer phases, but a proper distributed-target speckle model needs many scatterers per resolution cell and validated ensemble statistics. Real intensity includes source multilooking; the simulator does not reproduce its equivalent number of looks.

Spatial-block resampling estimates heterogeneity within one fixed real crop. It is not a flood-area accuracy confidence interval, and blocks may still be correlated. Multiple block sizes expose sensitivity. Independent validation requires temporally compatible reference labels and geographic holdouts.
