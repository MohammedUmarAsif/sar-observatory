Corrupted-input demonstration

experiments.generate inserts a NaN at raw[0,0]. The actual pipeline rejects it with echo contains NaN/Inf; stop before range compression. The website shows this recorded result, not a fake live server response. Scientific tests also reject real-valued and wrong-shape inputs.

Where simple diagnostics fail

Peak width cannot establish geometric accuracy: linear Doppler phase moves a sharp response. Peak-normalized plots cannot establish radiometric consistency: every image's strongest sample is mapped to 0 dB. A threshold-derived area cannot establish flood extent: the labels and physics of water/other dark surfaces are unresolved. These failures motivate combining physically distinct checks rather than one score.
