## Question

How do controlled platform-velocity mismatch and residual linear Doppler phase
affect point-target quality, and which automated diagnostics distinguish blur
from displacement? Engineering relevance: product validation needs independent
checks of impulse shape, geometric location and coherent response.

## Methods

One isolated point target, rectangular LFM chirp and finite aperture, flat Cartesian
geometry and stop-and-go sampling. Delays and two-way carrier phase are exact
within the model. Linear matched filtering is followed by direct time-domain
backprojection. Complex compressed samples are interpolated linearly; pulses
are coherently averaged. Main cases change one factor. An extended scene is
separate so neighboring responses do not contaminate isolated-target metrics.

Independent checks compare FFT filtering to direct convolution, backprojection
to a raw-echo matched inner product, and widths/sidelobes to analytic references.
Half-power crossings use linear power interpolation; sidelobe energy is measured
outside first local minima in x ±8 m and y ±30 m windows. Discretization allowances
are 0.10 m and 1.0 m for the respective full widths, not statistical error bars.

## Discussion and limitations

The experiment supports combining geometric and impulse-shape diagnostics:
sharpness does not establish geometric validity. Real imagery illustrates the
parallel inference problem: adaptive class separation does not establish stable
temporal water mapping. The two branches share disciplined validation, not one
numerical processor or mission configuration.

The simulator omits Earth curvature, antenna gains, path-loss/radiometric calibration,
atmosphere, motion during propagation and instrument transfer functions. It does
not ingest spaceborne raw formats. Linear interpolation and discrete windows limit
precision. Quality gates are educational choices, not mission requirements.
The real study has one fixed AOI, no contemporaneous independent labels, no measured
subpixel registration accuracy and no locally validated terrain/noise masks.
Season, wind, vegetation and other dark surfaces complicate candidate-water inference.
Otsu is not demonstrated as an improvement; spatial dependence remains after blocking.

Next justified steps: compatible reference labels and geographic holdouts;
independent co-registration checks; a controlled apodization study; a validated
fractional-delay interpolator and precision/runtime tradeoff. Avoid deep learning
until data and evaluation design actually justify it.
